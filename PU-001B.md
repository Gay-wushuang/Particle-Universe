## 4. Failure analysis and stop decision

All C4 trajectories remained finite and bounded. Nevertheless, the measured
energy-fluctuation amplitude did not exhibit a stable second-order scaling:
the adjacent-step slopes were approximately 0.609 and 1.759, and the global
log-log slope was 1.184. The corresponding `std(E)/dt^2` values were about
2.047, 0.781, and 0.660. Thus the result cannot support the frozen
`O(dt^2)` claim under the documented interpretation.

The run does not identify a unique cause. Plausible contributors include the
force discontinuity of energy-shifted variant A at the cutoff, cutoff-crossing
phase sensitivity, chaotic decorrelation, and the finite observation window.
Those possibilities do not authorize changing the potential, threshold,
metric, or physical time. C4 is therefore recorded as FAIL, and C5 was not
executed. Further experimental design or any physics-semantic change requires
Gate review.

This failure does not invalidate the earlier mathematical and deterministic
core tests, but it prevents claiming completion of the PU-001 numerical Gate.
